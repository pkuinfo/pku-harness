## 更新内容

- Copilot 在不停变化的页面上也能点按：轮播图、倒计时、实时刷新的页面不再让点击总是“界面已变化（STALE）”。只要要点的按钮还在、没有被别的可点击元素盖住，就会执行；按钮消失或被遮挡时仍会拒绝

## What's new

- Copilot can tap on pages that never hold still: auto-rotating banners, countdowns and live feeds no longer make taps fail as “the screen changed (STALE)”. A tap goes through as long as its button is still there and nothing tappable covers it; it is still refused when the button is gone or covered
