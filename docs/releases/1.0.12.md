## 更新内容

- 长任务可以反复自动压缩上下文，不再因为“过长”而失败；打开超出预算的旧对话时也会自动压缩
- Copilot 应用列表右侧加入完整的 A–Z 字母索引，中文应用按拼音首字母归类
- 语音输入：麦克风出错时会提示原因；修复快速取消再开始时录音混乱；修复刚启动时打开语音设置会覆盖已保存的服务
- 不可用的按钮重新显示为灰色
- 下载更新时划走面板会真正关闭面板，下载在后台继续
- 看板的“今天”卡片显示易读的日期；未改动的看板卡片跟随应用语言
- 语音识别服务归入“AI 服务”分组；错误信息更简洁

## What's new

- Long tasks compact their context as often as needed instead of failing as “too long”; an older chat over its budget compacts when you open it
- Copilot’s app list has a full A–Z index on the right; Chinese app names are filed by pinyin
- Voice input: microphone errors show their reason; a quick cancel-and-restart no longer garbles the recording; opening Voice input right after launch no longer overwrites your saved service
- Disabled buttons look disabled again
- Swiping the update panel away during a download really closes it; the download continues
- The dashboard’s Today card shows a readable date; unedited dashboard cards follow the app language
- Speech services are grouped under AI services; error messages are shorter
