## 更新内容

- Copilot 需要其他 App 时会直接询问你（仅本次 / 始终允许 / 不允许），不再中断任务
- 可选「允许 AI 查看截图」：在微信等不显示内容的 App 中，用截图和网格定位完成操作，每一步都需确认
- 浮动窗口在 PKU Harness 内自动缩成小圆球；离开 App 时的确认请求会以通知提醒
- 新手引导加入 Copilot 一步；设置页的输入框不再被键盘遮挡；开关即时响应
- 新增「关于」页面（版本、官网、作者、用户协议与隐私政策）和「触感反馈」开关
- 去掉应用内的文字识别模型，安装包更小；消息发送更流畅

## What's new

- When Copilot needs another app it asks you right there (allow once, always allow or don't allow) instead of stopping
- Optional "Let the AI see screenshots": works in apps like WeChat that hide their contents, using a gridded screenshot; every step asks you first
- The floating window shrinks to a ball while you are in PKU Harness; requests that wait while you are away come as a notification
- Onboarding includes a Copilot step; fields in setup no longer hide behind the keyboard; switches respond instantly
- New About page (version, website, author, terms and privacy) and a Haptic feedback switch
- No on-device text-recognition model, so a smaller APK; sending messages is smoother
