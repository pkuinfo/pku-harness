## 更新内容

- 用 Copilot 购物时，它会帮你选好商品、地址和选项，停在结算页交给你：由你点「提交订单」并付款。此前由 Copilot 提交订单，在美团等 App 中会出现“参数错误”
- 交给你操作的步骤完成后，点悬浮窗中的「完成 · 继续」会直接回到 PKU harness，查看 Copilot 接下来的进展
- 新的可选设置步骤“显示在其他应用上层”：开启后，三星等手机录屏时也能录下 Copilot 的悬浮窗

## What's new

- When Copilot shops for you, it picks the items, address and options, then stops on the checkout page and hands it to you: you tap Submit order and pay. Before, Copilot submitted the order itself, which failed with a "parameter error" in apps such as Meituan
- After a step you took over, tapping Done · continue in the floating window brings you straight back to PKU harness to follow Copilot's progress
- New optional setup step "Display over other apps": with it on, screen recordings on Samsung and similar phones include Copilot's floating window
