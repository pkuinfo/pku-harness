## 更新内容

- Copilot 每次操作前都会检查无障碍权限：被关闭（在系统设置里关掉，或被系统停掉）时会自动重新开启并继续任务，不再中途停止，也不会把它误判为“受保护页面”。无法自动开启时会直接告诉你去 系统设置 → 无障碍 打开
- 欢迎页的 Copilot 一步列出它需要的权限（手机助手、无障碍、允许的 App），说明无障碍的用途，并可一键开启
- 授权弹窗和底部面板出现时自动收起键盘，不再挡住按钮

## What's new

- Copilot checks its accessibility permission before every action: if it was switched off (in system settings, or stopped by the system) it turns it back on and carries on with the task, instead of stopping or mistaking it for a “protected screen”. If it can’t, it tells you to turn it on in Settings → Accessibility
- The welcome screen’s Copilot step lists what Copilot needs (phone helper, accessibility, allowed apps), explains what accessibility is for, and can turn it on in one tap
- The keyboard closes when a permission prompt or bottom sheet appears, so it no longer covers the buttons
