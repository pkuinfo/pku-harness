## 更新内容

- 移除上一版新增的两个「Copilot · 微信」无障碍服务，恢复为一个
- Copilot 遇到微信拦截自动操作（界面读不到、截图被禁、输入无效）时，会直接告诉你是微信在阻止自动操作、需要你自己完成这一步，不再误报为“受保护页面”或反复重试

## What's new

- Removed the two “Copilot · WeChat” accessibility services added in the previous version; there is one service again
- When WeChat blocks automated operation (nothing readable, screenshots blocked, typing ignored), Copilot now says plainly that WeChat is blocking automation and that you need to do that step yourself, instead of calling it a “protected screen” or retrying
