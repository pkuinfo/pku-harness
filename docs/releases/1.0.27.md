## 更新内容

- Copilot 更快：点了没有反应、或页面一直在动时，不再每步多等两秒多；发给 AI 的屏幕内容精简了一半左右，每一步思考更快
- 在微信小程序里付款时，交给你操作的是小程序本身（或商家打开的支付 App），不再是进入小程序之前的微信页面
- 三星等手机录屏时可以录下 Copilot 的悬浮窗（需在 Copilot 权限中允许“显示在其他应用上层”，三星称“显示在顶部”）
- 设置页第一项即是 Copilot，不必再从插件里找
- 点“立即更新”后，更新窗口会显示下载进度；关掉窗口也会在后台继续下载，下载完成时若窗口仍开着会直接打开安装

## What's new

- Copilot is faster: no more two-plus extra seconds per step when a tap changes nothing or a page keeps moving, and the screen sent to the AI is about half the size, so each step thinks faster
- When paying inside a WeChat mini program, Copilot hands you the mini program itself (or the payment app the shop opened), not the WeChat page from before the mini program
- Screen recordings on Samsung and similar phones include Copilot's floating window (allow "Display over other apps", called "Appear on top" on Samsung, in Copilot's permissions)
- Copilot is the first row in Settings, no longer only under Plugins
- After you tap Update now, the update sheet shows the download's progress; closing it lets the download go on in the background, and if the sheet is still open when it finishes, the installer opens
