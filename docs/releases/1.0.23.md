## 更新内容

- 回复语言跟随你的消息：用中文问就用中文答，即使应用界面是英文
- 选择模型时可以设置思考强度：自动（规划任务时认真想，操作手机时快速想）、低、高、最高
- 助手操作手机时更快：每一步的操作思考更少
- 付款只需你来付：助手完成下单前的所有步骤，弹出支付宝或微信支付的付款界面时再交给你
- 助手操作手机期间在本应用里打字，三星键盘不再需要每个键按两次
- 浮窗：缩小后的小球更小，下拉通知栏或打开最近任务时不会再自己展开；浮窗两侧更紧凑；在本应用里不再显示浮窗
- 对话请求更精简，缓存命中更高

## What's new

- Replies follow the language you write in: ask in Chinese and the answer is in Chinese, even with the app in English
- Choose how hard the AI thinks in the model picker: Auto (careful when planning, quick when operating your phone), Low, High or Max
- Copilot is faster: less thinking between steps on your phone
- You only pay: Copilot does every step of an order and hands over when the Alipay or WeChat Pay sheet opens
- Typing in PKU harness while Copilot works no longer needs two taps per key on Samsung Keyboard
- Floating panel: a smaller ball that stays a ball when you pull down notifications or open Recents; tighter sides; hidden inside PKU harness
- Leaner requests with better prompt caching
