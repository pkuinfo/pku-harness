## 更新内容

- 离开应用或锁屏时，助手继续操作手机：任务进行中屏幕保持变暗常亮；按电源键熄屏会暂停，点亮屏幕后自动继续（无需解锁）
- 助手需要你确认或接手时，会点亮屏幕并在锁屏上通知你，处理完通知才消失；接手最多等你 30 分钟
- 新版本在后台下载：点“立即更新”后弹窗关闭，下载进度显示在通知栏，不打扰你使用应用；下载完成后点通知即可安装
- 新设置“在 Wi-Fi 下自动下载更新”（默认开启）：连上 Wi-Fi 时自动下载新版本，安装仍由你决定

## What's new

- Copilot keeps working when you leave the app or lock the phone: the screen stays dimmed on during a task; pressing the power button pauses it, and it resumes when the screen turns on (no unlock needed)
- When Copilot needs your approval or a handoff, it wakes the screen and shows a lock-screen notification that stays until you act; handoffs wait up to 30 minutes
- New versions download in the background: tap Update now and the sheet closes, progress shows in the notification bar, and a notification lets you install when it's done
- New setting "Download updates on Wi-Fi" (on by default): new versions download by themselves on Wi-Fi; installing is still your call
