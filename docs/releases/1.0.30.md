## 更新内容

- 树洞有了完整的界面：最新 / 关注两个标签，下拉刷新、向下滚动无限加载；可以直接搜索（不必通过 AI）；帖子显示图片，点开可左右翻看大图
- 树洞帖子页：评论按发言人用不同颜色区分，洞主单独标注，引用的回复会显示原文；点一条评论即可引用回复，在底部输入框直接发送
- Copilot 下单会一直做到订单页（能看到商品、价格和「去支付」按钮）再交给你，「去结算」「立即购买」这类只是打开订单页的按钮由 Copilot 自己点；你只需要点支付并完成验证
- 微信小程序交接时重新加载后，Copilot 按刚才点过的按钮直接把它点回订单页，约 1.5 秒（以前要等模型重新摸索，十几秒）；这期间悬浮窗不会挡住点按

## What's new

- Treehole has a full interface: Latest and Following tabs, pull to refresh and an endless feed that loads as you scroll; search it directly (no AI needed); posts show their pictures, and tapping one opens a full-size viewer you can swipe through
- Treehole threads: each person in a thread gets their own colour, the original poster is marked, and quoted replies show the text they quote; tap a comment to quote it and reply from the box at the bottom
- Copilot now carries an order all the way to the order page (product, price and the Pay button) before handing it to you; buttons that only open the order page, such as Checkout or Buy now, it taps itself. You only tap Pay and confirm
- When a WeChat mini program reloads during the handoff, Copilot taps straight back to the order page using the buttons it pressed before, in about 1.5 s (it used to work its way back with the model, over ten seconds); the floating window no longer blocks those taps
