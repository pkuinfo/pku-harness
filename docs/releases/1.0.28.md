## 更新内容

- 修复部分手机（如三星）上 Copilot 运行时看不到悬浮窗和悬浮球的问题：系统没有显示悬浮窗时，Copilot 会自动换一种方式显示。打开某些会隐藏悬浮窗的页面时也会自动切换
- Copilot 设置页更紧凑：权限列表与上方内容对齐，行距更合理，「启用 Copilot」和「截图」合并为一组
- 把微信小程序交给你时，部分手机（如三星）会让小程序重新加载、回到首页。Copilot 现在会把商品留在购物车里，并在悬浮窗中提示你去购物车结算

## What's new

- Fixed Copilot's floating window and ball not showing on some phones such as Samsung: when the system doesn't show the window, Copilot shows it another way, and it switches automatically on screens that hide floating windows
- A tidier Copilot settings page: the permission list lines up with the rows above, spacing is tighter, and Enable Copilot and Screenshots share one card
- On some phones such as Samsung, a WeChat mini program reloads to its home page when Copilot hands it to you. Copilot now leaves the items in the shop's cart, and the floating window tells you to check out from the cart
