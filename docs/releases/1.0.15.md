## 更新内容

- Copilot 按截图点按不再被“画面已变化（STALE）”拒绝：淘宝搜索提示轮换、广告轮播、倒计时不停刷新的页面也能直接点下去。仍会拦下的只有：不在允许范围内的应用、受保护的画面、密码输入框，以及付款位置（会交给你确认）

## What's new

- Copilot’s screenshot taps are no longer refused as “the screen changed (STALE)”: they land even on pages that redraw every second (Taobao’s rotating search hints, banners, countdowns). The only taps still stopped are in apps outside its allowed list, on protected screens, on password fields, and on payment spots (handed to you)
