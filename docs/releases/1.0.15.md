## 更新内容

- 对话可以直接读文件：PDF、Word、Excel、PowerPoint 在手机上本地读成文字（不上传文件），长文档 AI 会按需翻页和搜索；音频文件由语音输入服务转写。扫描版 PDF、有密码或损坏的文件会说明原因
- Copilot 完全接管手机：不再因“画面已变化（STALE）”拒绝点按，按钮原位置变了也会按原位置点；受保护的画面（如淘宝搜索页）可以读取和操作，只是不截图；修复误报“不在允许的应用内”；应用冷启动慢时会多等几次。仍会停下的只有：允许范围外的应用、密码输入框和付款（交给你确认）
- AI 回答出现正文后，前面的工具调用记录自动折叠
- 粘贴文本的预览才显示“移回输入框”

## What's new

- Chats can read files: PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint are read into text on the phone (the file is not uploaded), and the AI pages through and searches long documents as needed; audio files are transcribed by the voice-input service. Scanned PDFs, password-protected and damaged files are refused with a reason
- Copilot takes full control of the phone: no more “the screen changed (STALE)” refusals, and a button that moved is still tapped where it was; protected screens (such as Taobao’s search page) can be read and operated, just not screenshotted; fixed false “outside the allowed apps” errors; it waits longer for slow app starts. It still stops only for apps outside its allowed list, password fields and payments (handed to you)
- Tool-call records fold away once the answer’s text appears
- Only pasted text offers “Move back to input” in its preview
