## 更新内容

- Copilot 可以直接读取并操作微信界面，无需开启截图；截图方式保留为备用
- 打开 App 后若还在启动画面，Copilot 会等它加载完成再判断
- 升级或重启手机后，Copilot 会在下次任务时自动恢复无障碍服务和连接
- 对话上下文用量重新计入工具定义，估算更准确

## What's new

- Copilot reads and operates WeChat directly, without screenshots; the screenshot mode stays as a backup
- When an app is still on its splash screen, Copilot waits for it to load before deciding anything
- After an update or a phone restart, Copilot turns its accessibility service and connection back on by itself at the next task
- The context meter counts tool definitions again, so its estimate is accurate
