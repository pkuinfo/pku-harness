# Code of Conduct

PKU Harness follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be inclusive, and argue about ideas, not people. No harassment, no personal
attacks, and never publish other people's private information. On a campus project that
especially includes names, student IDs and campus accounts.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers of the [pkuinfo](https://github.com/pkuinfo)
organisation through a private GitHub message, or by email to the address on the organisation
profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Depending on the case, maintainers may respond with a
reminder, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
